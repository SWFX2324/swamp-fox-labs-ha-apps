How tracking works
  • Search by flight number (AA1920), callsign (AAL1920), registration (N195UW) or ICAO hex (A17C95). Ticket numbers for most US airlines are converted for you.
  • One match is tracked automatically: shown alone and followed, anywhere in the world.
  • Updates every 15 s zoomed in, or every 60 s zoomed out or while on the ground. The map by itself requests no aircraft data.
  • Tracking ends by itself when the flight lands, when the aircraft starts a different flight, or after 2 hours with no signal. It’s saved in this browser for up to 12 hours.
  • Only positions actually received are shown: no schedules, routes or arrival times. Coverage thins out over oceans and remote areas.
  • Codeshare? Use the operating airline’s flight number. Regional flights often broadcast as SKW, RPA, ENY or EDV.
