How Mesh Hub works
  • Your radios connect over your network: a Meshtastic node with Wi-Fi or Ethernet (port 4403) and a MeshCore companion radio running Wi-Fi firmware (port 5000). Set their addresses on the app’s Configuration tab.
  • The two networks can’t hear each other. Each tab shows one of them; All shows both, coloured green for Meshtastic and blue for MeshCore.
  • Public nodes come from meshmap.net (Meshtastic, via the public MQTT server) and the MeshCore map, within the radius you set around your Home Assistant home. They’re hollow on the map.
  • Messages are kept in the app (the newest 1,000 per network), so they’re here when you come back. A tick means a node passed your message on; two mean the recipient confirmed it.
  • A Meshtastic node takes one Wi-Fi app connection at a time. If the phone app connects over Wi-Fi it can push Mesh Hub off; over Bluetooth both work at once.
