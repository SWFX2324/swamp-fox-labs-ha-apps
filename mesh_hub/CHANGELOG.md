# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: a Meshtastic node and a MeshCore companion radio over Wi-Fi (TCP) on
  one page, with **All**, **Meshtastic** and **MeshCore** tabs.
- Your radios' connection, battery, firmware and radio settings.
- A map and a sortable node list with hops, SNR, battery, distance and last heard.
- Channels and direct messages, with sending, delivery ticks and SNR and hops on every
  message. Messages are kept across restarts.
- Public nodes near your home from meshmap.net and the MeshCore map.
