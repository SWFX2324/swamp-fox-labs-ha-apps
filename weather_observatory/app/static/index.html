Sources & data notes

Current conditions and forecasts come from your Home Assistant weather entity; they are model estimates, not measurements at your home. Past 24h is what Home Assistant actually recorded, not a backfilled forecast. Station data are measured at a nearby NWS station, not at your home. Radar shows RainViewer’s past frames with each frame’s time. Air quality is a CAMS model estimate via Open-Meteo. Public services receive only your area rounded to 0.1° (about 11 km), never your exact location; map and radar tiles are loaded by your browser. Missing values stay blank: zero appears only when a source reports zero.
